## RECITALS

**WHEREAS** The Aquatic Coach operates The Aquatic Coach Platform, providing evidence-based endurance coaching integrated with athlete training data;

**WHEREAS** the Shop sells gravel bicycles and associated training technology and wishes to offer customers a value-added coaching package;

**WHEREAS** the Parties wish to provide customers who purchase a Qualifying Bundle with eight (8) weeks of complimentary co-branded platform access, followed by an optional paid subscription with revenue sharing;

**WHEREAS** the Parties recognise that the Shop’s reputation is of paramount importance and agree to uphold strict co-brand and communication standards;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** **“Activation Code”** means the unique code issued by the Shop at sale for customer platform registration.

**1.2** **“Agreement”** means this Cycling Shop Partnership Agreement and its schedules.

**1.3** **“Co-Branded Materials”** means customer-facing assets bearing both Parties’ branding, approved pursuant to Section 8.

**1.4** **“Consultation”** means the initial 45–60 minute remote coaching intake session.

**1.5** **“Conversion”** means a Referred Customer’s opt-in to a paid monthly subscription after the Free Period.

**1.6** **“Free Period”** means eight (8) weeks of complimentary platform access commencing on successful registration.

**1.7** **“Gravel Bike”** means a new bicycle categorised by the Shop as a gravel bike in its point-of-sale system.

**1.8** **“Heart Rate Monitor”** means a chest strap or validated optical HRM sold by the Shop and listed in Schedule B.

**1.9** **“Monthly Training Fee”** means the standard monthly subscription fee for continued platform access, currently **€[MONTHLY_FEE]** excluding applicable taxes, as may be updated with thirty (30) days’ notice to the Shop.

**1.10** **“Platform”** means The Aquatic Coach Platform and related coaching services.

**1.11** **“Qualifying Bundle”** means a single transaction including a Gravel Bike, Sports Watch, and Heart Rate Monitor as defined in Section 3.

**1.12** **“Referred Customer”** means an individual who registers using a valid Activation Code within thirty (30) days of a Qualifying Bundle purchase.

**1.13** **“Revenue Share”** means the Shop’s percentage of Monthly Training Fees from Converted customers under Section 6.

**1.14** **“Sports Watch”** means a GPS-enabled sports watch sold by the Shop and listed in Schedule B.

**1.15** **“Term”** means the period specified in Section 12.

## 2. PARTNERSHIP MODEL

**2.1** The Shop promotes and activates the Qualifying Bundle offer at point of sale. The Aquatic Coach delivers the Free Period coaching services and ongoing platform access for Converted customers.

**2.2** This Agreement does not create employment, franchise, or exclusive agency. The Shop is an independent retail partner.

**2.3** Unless Schedule C specifies exclusivity by geography, the Shop’s partnership is **non-exclusive**. The Aquatic Coach may partner with other retailers.

---

## 3. QUALIFYING BUNDLE

**3.1** A Qualifying Bundle must include all three components purchased together (or via an approved bundle SKU):

(a) one (1) Gravel Bike;  
(b) one (1) Sports Watch; and  
(c) one (1) Heart Rate Monitor.

**3.2** Minimum bundle value: **€[MIN_BUNDLE_VALUE]** excluding VAT, unless waived in writing.

**3.3** One Free Period per Referred Customer per Qualifying Bundle. Duplicate activations on the same customer within twenty-four (24) months require written approval.

**3.4** If the Qualifying Bundle is returned or refunded, the Shop shall notify The Aquatic Coach within forty-eight (48) hours and the Free Period access shall terminate pro-rata.

---

## 4. FREE PERIOD — AQUATIC COACH OBLIGATIONS

**4.1** For each valid Referred Customer, The Aquatic Coach shall provide during the Free Period:

(a) co-branded onboarding per Section 8;  
(b) one (1) Consultation within fourteen (14) days of registration;  
(c) an eight (8) week personalised training plan;  
(d) individually prescribed weekly workouts;  
(e) readiness-aware session guidance where data is available;  
(f) one (1) mid-program check-in at approximately week four (4);  
(g) an end-of-trial summary at week eight (8) with continuation options.

**4.2** The Aquatic Coach shall deliver services with reasonable skill and care. No guarantee of specific performance outcomes is made or implied.

**4.3** The Aquatic Coach shall respond to Referred Customer coaching support requests within **two (2) business days** during the Free Period.

**4.4** Estimated commercial value of the Free Period per customer: **€[FREE_PERIOD_VALUE]** for reference.

---

## 5. SHOP OBLIGATIONS

**5.1** Issue a unique **Activation Code** at checkout for each Qualifying Bundle sale.

**5.2** Provide the customer with Co-Branded Materials (activation card or QR) and direct them to register within thirty (30) days.

**5.3** Ensure sales staff use only **approved** descriptions from the Shop Sales One-Pager (Schedule D). Staff shall not make prohibited claims listed in Section 8.4.

**5.4** Display Co-Branded Materials at point of sale in gravel / technology retail areas within fourteen (14) days of go-live.

**5.5** Notify The Aquatic Coach of bundle returns/refunds within forty-eight (48) hours.

**5.6** Participate in co-brand approval per Section 8 before customer-facing launch.

**5.7** Designate a **Partnership Contact** for operational and reputation escalations.

---

## 6. CONTINUATION, PRICING & REVENUE SHARE

**6.1** At the end of the Free Period, Referred Customers may **opt in** to a paid subscription. No automatic billing without explicit customer consent.

**6.2** **Revenue Share split** on Monthly Training Fees from Converted Referred Customers:

| Party | Share |
|-------|-------|
| The Aquatic Coach | **[AQUATIC_COACH_SHARE]%** |
| Shop | **[SHOP_SHARE]%** |

**6.3** Revenue Share applies for **twelve (12) months** from the Referred Customer’s first paid month, unless Schedule C states otherwise.

**6.4** **Payout:** Shop share calculated monthly; paid by the **15th** of the following month via SEPA transfer. Minimum payout **€50** (balances roll forward).

**6.5** **Clawback:** If a Converted customer receives a full refund within thirty (30) days of a paid month, Shop share for that month is reversed.

**6.6** **Reporting:** The Aquatic Coach provides monthly statements: activations, active Free Period users, Conversions, and Revenue Share due.

**6.7** **Attribution:** Only Referred Customers who register with a valid Activation Code within thirty (30) days qualify for Revenue Share.

**6.8** The Shop shall not offer competing structured coaching subscriptions bundled with bike sales without thirty (30) days’ written notice to The Aquatic Coach.

---

## 7. CONSULTATION & INDIVIDUAL WORKOUTS

**7.1** Consultations and workouts are prescribed by The Aquatic Coach (human coach oversight with Section 11 protocol support).

**7.2** Shop staff may attend Consultations only with Referred Customer consent and shall not provide coaching or medical advice.

**7.3** Workouts are individualised to the Referred Customer’s baseline, goals, and equipment. Generic unmodified templates alone do not satisfy Section 4.1(d).

**7.4** The Shop may refer customers to The Aquatic Coach for setup questions beyond retail product operation; The Aquatic Coach handles coaching scope.

---

## 8. REPUTATION, CO-BRANDING & COMMUNICATIONS

**8.1 Reputation priority.** Both Parties acknowledge the Shop’s reputation is a **primary asset**. The Aquatic Coach shall act to protect it in all customer interactions and Co-Branded Materials.

**8.2 Co-brand approval.** No Co-Branded Materials go live without the Shop’s written approval (email sufficient). The Shop shall respond within **five (5) business days** or be deemed to approve if only minor template population changes are requested.

**8.3 Shop rights.**

(a) **Pause activations** — The Shop may pause issuing new Activation Codes immediately upon written notice if a reputation or customer-safety concern arises, pending joint review within five (5) business days.  
(b) **Branding removal** — The Shop may request temporary removal of its branding from an individual customer’s platform view during an active dispute.  
(c) **Material rejection** — The Shop may reject Co-Branded Materials that misrepresent the Shop or overpromise outcomes.

**8.4 Prohibited claims.** Neither Party shall state or imply in Co-Branded Materials or sales conversations:

(a) guaranteed race results, times, weight loss, or injury prevention;  
(b) medical diagnosis or replacement of medical/physiotherapy care;  
(c) “free forever” coaching;  
(d) automatic subscription without opt-in; or  
(e) any claim not on the approved Shop Sales One-Pager.

**8.5 Complaint handling.**

(a) The Aquatic Coach acknowledges coaching complaints within **twenty-four (24) hours**;  
(b) the Shop is notified same day if the complaint relates to retail messaging or co-branding;  
(c) Parties offer a joint resolution call within **five (5) business days** when requested.

**8.6** The Aquatic Coach shall not use the Shop’s name in external advertising beyond the partnership without consent, except factual “official retail partner” listings.

---

## 9. DATA PROTECTION & CONFIDENTIALITY

**9.1** Each Party complies with GDPR and applicable data protection law.

**9.2** The Aquatic Coach is controller of Referred Customer training and health data on the Platform. The Shop receives only: activation, trial status, Conversion status, and aggregate Revenue Share data — not individual health metrics without customer consent.

**9.3** Confidential Information (commercial terms, unreleased pricing, customer lists beyond attribution) shall not be disclosed without consent, except as required by law.

**9.4** Confidentiality survives **two (2) years** after termination.

**10.3** The Shop grants The Aquatic Coach a limited licence to use the Shop logo solely in Co-Branded Materials and partner listings approved under Section 8.

**10.4** The Aquatic Coach retains all rights in the Platform, coaching protocols, and training content.

**11.1** Each Party warrants it has authority to enter this Agreement.

**11.2** Referred Customers participate in training at their own risk. The Shop warrants it sells fit-for-purpose hardware and provides accurate product information for devices sold.

**11.3** The Aquatic Coach’s total liability under this Agreement is limited to **Revenue Share paid to the Shop in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim** or **€[LIABILITY_CAP]**, whichever is greater, except for gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

**11.4** Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

**11.5** The Shop indemnifies The Aquatic Coach against claims arising from Shop sales misrepresentations, non-approved claims, or Shop breach, except where caused by The Aquatic Coach’s materials or coaching delivery.

**11.6** The Aquatic Coach indemnifies the Shop against claims arising from coaching negligence or non-approved Aquatic Coach marketing using the Shop’s name, except where caused by Shop non-approved claims.

---

## 12. TERM & TERMINATION

**12.1 Term.** Begins on the Effective Date and continues for **twelve (12) months**, auto-renewing for twelve (12) month periods unless either Party gives **sixty (60) days’ written notice** before expiry.

**12.2 Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate on **sixty (60) days’ written notice**.

**12.3 Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately on material breach uncured within **fourteen (14) days** of notice, or immediately for fraud, reputational harm, or unlawful conduct.

**12.4 Effect of termination.**

(a) New Activation Codes cease on termination date;  
(b) in-progress Free Periods continue to week eight (8) for already-registered Referred Customers;  
(c) Revenue Share continues for existing Converted customers until the end of their twelve (12) month share window;  
(d) Co-brand licence ends except for archival customer materials that cannot reasonably be withdrawn;  
(e) Sections 6.5, 9, 10, 11, and 13 survive.
