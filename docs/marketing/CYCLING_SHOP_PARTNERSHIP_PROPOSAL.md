## 1. Executive Summary

The Aquatic Coach Platform Cycling Shop Partnership is a **retail-aligned coaching offer** designed for bike shops that sell gravel bikes with supporting training technology. When a customer purchases a **qualifying bundle** — gravel bike, sports watch, and heart rate monitor — they receive **eight (8) weeks of complimentary access** to The Aquatic Coach Platform, presented in **co-branded packaging** that reflects the bike shop’s identity.

During the free period, the cyclist receives a structured onboarding consultation, a personalised training plan, and individually prescribed workouts aligned to their new equipment and goals. If the cyclist chooses to continue after eight weeks, **monthly subscription fees are shared** between The Aquatic Coach and the partner shop under an agreed revenue-split model.

**The partner shop’s reputation is treated as a first-order priority.** The Aquatic Coach will not publish content, make claims, or deliver coaching experiences that could reflect poorly on the shop. All customer-facing materials, onboarding language, and ongoing communication are subject to co-brand approval and quality standards defined in this proposal.

## 3. Qualifying Purchase — What Triggers the Offer

### 3.1 Bundle definition

The complimentary 8-week platform access activates when a customer purchases **all three** of the following in a single transaction (or approved bundle SKU) from the partner shop:

| Component | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Gravel bike** | New gravel bike (complete bike or frameset + build meeting shop’s gravel category definition) |
| **Sports watch** | GPS-enabled sports watch suitable for cycling (brand/model list in Schedule A or shop catalogue) |
| **Heart rate monitor** | Chest strap or validated optical HRM compatible with watch and Intervals.icu / Garmin ecosystem |

Optional add-ons ( shoes, pedals, sensors ) do not replace any of the three required items.

### 3.2 Minimum transaction value

Recommended minimum bundle value: **€[MIN_BUNDLE_VALUE]** (excl. VAT). The Aquatic Coach may approve exceptions for shop-specific promotions with 14 days’ written notice.

### 3.3 Eligibility

- **One activation per customer** per qualifying purchase (no duplicate trials on repeat bike purchases within 24 months unless approved)
- Customer must be **18+** and medically cleared for endurance training (self-declaration at onboarding)
- Offer valid only at **authorised partner locations** listed in the Partnership Agreement
- Customer must **activate within 30 days** of purchase

## 4. What the Cyclist Receives — 8-Week Complimentary Access

### 4.1 Co-branded platform experience

The cyclist’s onboarding and in-app experience carries **dual branding**:

| Element | Branding |
|---------|----------|
| Welcome email / activation link | Shop logo + The Aquatic Coach Platform |
| Onboarding portal | Co-branded header; shop name as “Official Retail Partner” |
| Consultation confirmation | Signed off by both brands |
| Weekly summary (optional PDF) | Shop logo in footer with partnership credit |
| Certificate / milestone graphic at week 8 | Co-branded completion asset for shop to share (with customer consent) |

The shop approves logo usage, colour placement, and customer-facing copy before launch. The Aquatic Coach supplies templates; the shop may request reasonable brand adjustments.

### 4.2 Included services during the 8-week period

| Service | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Initial consultation** | 45–60 minute remote consultation (video or structured async) covering goals, experience, equipment setup, and data connectivity |
| **Personalised training plan** | 8-week structured plan aligned to gravel riding, fitness baseline, and available training time |
| **Individual workouts** | Prescribed sessions (e.g., endurance, tempo, skills/endurance mix, recovery) — not generic templates |
| **Intervals.icu integration** | Setup guidance for watch/HRM data flow; readiness-aware session guidance |
| **Readiness decisions** | Go / modify / skip recommendations based on load, HRV, RHR, sleep, and recovery where data available |
| **Pre- and post-session reporting** | Briefings and feedback for prescribed sessions |
| **Mid-program check-in** | 20–30 minute review at week 4 (adjust plan if needed) |
| **End-of-trial summary** | Week 8 progress summary and continuation options |

### 4.3 What is not included in the free period (unless add-on agreed)

- On-site bike fitting tied to coaching (shop service; separate fee)
- Nutrition coaching beyond basic session fuelling guidance
- Race-specific multi-month periodisation beyond the 8-week block
- Unlimited ad-hoc messaging (standard response windows apply)

## 5. Consultation & Workout Creation — How It Works

### 5.1 Consultation flow

```
Purchase → Activation code at till → Customer registers → 
Book consultation (within 7 days) → Data/connectivity check → 
Plan published (within 5 days of consultation) → Weekly workouts released
```

**Consultation covers:**

1. Riding history and gravel experience
2. Goals for the 8-week block (fitness, first event, distance target, technique)
3. Weekly time availability and indoor/outdoor preference
4. Watch and HRM setup; Garmin / Intervals.icu connection
5. Injury history and current health flags
6. Explanation of readiness-led coaching — realistic expectations set explicitly

### 5.2 Individual workout prescription

Workouts are **created for the individual**, not copied from a generic library without modification. Each week includes:

- **Session objective** (e.g., aerobic base, sweet spot, long gravel endurance)
- **Duration and intensity** aligned to customer zones once established
- **Equipment notes** (HR cap, power if available, terrain suggestions)
- **Readiness modifier** — session adjusts if data indicates fatigue or overload

The Aquatic Coach uses the Section 11 AI Coach Protocol with **human coach oversight** on consultation, plan structure, and flagged sessions.

### 5.3 Shop involvement in consultation (optional)

The shop may attend the initial consultation (with customer consent) to:

- Confirm bike setup and sensor pairing
- Reinforce local routes or shop group rides
- Strengthen the retail relationship

The shop does **not** deliver medical or coaching advice unless separately qualified. Coaching liability remains with The Aquatic Coach.

## 6. Continuation & Revenue Share (After Week 8)

### 6.1 Conversion path

At week 8, the cyclist chooses:

| Option | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| **Continue on platform** | Monthly subscription begins; revenue share applies (Section 6.2) |
| **Pause / decline** | Access ends; no charge; shop may still offer retail follow-up |
| **Upgrade** | Extended plan (3 / 6 / 12 months) — revenue share applies to ongoing monthly equivalent |

The customer is **never auto-billed without explicit opt-in** (GDPR / consumer protection compliant).

### 6.2 Revenue share model

If the cyclist continues after the complimentary period, **monthly training fees are split** between The Aquatic Coach and the partner shop:

| Party | Share (default) |
|-------|-----------------|
| **The Aquatic Coach** | **[AQUATIC_COACH_SHARE]%** (coaching delivery, platform, support) |
| **Partner shop** | **[SHOP_SHARE]%** (customer acquisition, retail relationship, local trust) |

**Example (illustrative):**

- Standard monthly fee: **€[MONTHLY_FEE]**
- Shop share at 30%: **€[SHOP_EARNINGS]** per retained customer per month
- Aquatic Coach share at 70%: **€[ACP_EARNINGS]** per retained customer per month

Share percentages are fixed in the Partnership Agreement and apply for **twelve (12) months** from the customer’s first paid month, unless otherwise agreed.

### 6.3 Payment mechanics

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Reporting** | Monthly statement of active referred subscribers attributed to the shop |
| **Payout** | Shop share paid by **15th of following month** via bank transfer |
| **Minimum payout** | €50; balances roll forward |
| **Clawback** | If customer refunds within 30 days of first payment, shop share for that month is reversed |
| **Attribution** | Customer must register with shop-issued activation code for revenue share eligibility |

### 6.4 Long-term customer relationship

- The Aquatic Coach owns the coaching relationship and platform delivery
- The shop remains credited as the **introducing retail partner** in the customer record
- The shop may contact retained customers for **legitimate retail purposes** (service, accessories, events) per its own CRM and GDPR policies — not for competing coaching offers

## 7. Bike Shop Reputation — Our Highest Priority

The Aquatic Coach recognises that a cycling shop’s reputation is built over years of trusted advice. This partnership is designed to **protect and enhance** that reputation — never exploit it.

### 7.1 Reputation principles

| Principle | Commitment |
|-----------|------------|
| **No overpromising** | We do not guarantee race results, weight loss, or injury prevention in shop-branded materials |
| **Accurate positioning** | Coaching is evidence-based and data-informed; we say what the platform does and does not do |
| **Co-brand control** | Shop approves customer-facing co-branded assets before use; reasonable edit requests honoured within 5 business days |
| **Escalation path** | Dedicated partnership line for reputation concerns — response within 24 hours |
| **Right to pause** | Shop may pause new activations immediately if a customer complaint or brand risk arises, pending review |
| **Staff training** | The Aquatic Coach provides a **30-minute shop staff briefing** document/video so sales staff describe the offer accurately |
| **No disparagement** | Neither party publicly criticises the other; disputes resolved privately first |

### 7.2 Prohibited shop-facing claims (staff must not say)

- “This will make you race-ready in 8 weeks guaranteed”
- “Medical-grade heart monitoring” (unless device is medically certified)
- “Replace your doctor / physio”
- “Free coaching forever”
- Any claim not in the approved **Shop Sales One-Pager**

### 7.3 Approved shop-facing claims

- “Eight weeks of structured gravel training included with your bike, watch, and heart rate monitor bundle”
- “Personal consultation and individual workouts via The Aquatic Coach Platform”
- “Readiness-led coaching that uses your watch and heart rate data”
- “Continue after 8 weeks if you choose — no automatic charge”

### 7.4 Customer complaint handling

1. Customer contacts shop or The Aquatic Coach
2. The Aquatic Coach acknowledges within **24 hours**
3. Shop notified of coaching-related complaints same day
4. Joint resolution call offered if complaint implicates retail messaging
5. Shop may request removal of its branding from an individual customer’s view during dispute resolution

## 8. Partner Shop Obligations

### 8.1 Point of sale

| Obligation | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| **Bundle merchandising** | Display co-branded point-of-sale material at gravel / tech display |
| **Activation code issuance** | Provide unique code at checkout for each qualifying sale |
| **Accurate description** | Staff use approved one-pager only |
| **Customer handoff** | Direct buyer to activation URL or QR code before leaving store |
| **Returns** | Notify The Aquatic Coach within 48 hours if bundle is returned; trial access revoked pro-rata |

### 8.2 Optional marketing (recommended, not mandatory)

- One **in-store event** per quarter (gravel demo + platform intro)
- Social post when partnership launches (template supplied)
- Inclusion in shop newsletter (copy supplied)

No mandatory weekly posting — unlike the corporate team program. Reputation-sensitive, low-pressure retail integration is the default.

### 8.3 Data & privacy

- Shop does not receive customer health data without explicit customer consent
- Shop receives only: activation status, trial completion, conversion yes/no, revenue share totals
- Both parties GDPR-compliant
