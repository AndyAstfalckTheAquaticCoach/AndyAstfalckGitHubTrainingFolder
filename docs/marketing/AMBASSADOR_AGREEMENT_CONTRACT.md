## RECITALS

**WHEREAS** The Aquatic Coach operates The Aquatic Coach Platform, providing evidence-based endurance and aquatic coaching services integrated with athlete training data;

**WHEREAS** the Ambassador is an active subscriber and user of the Platform who wishes to participate in the Ambassador Marketing Program;

## 2. APPOINTMENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Subject to this Agreement, The Aquatic Coach appoints the Ambassador as a non-exclusive, independent ambassador for the Term. The Ambassador accepts the appointment.

**2.2 Independent contractor.** The Ambassador is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency. The Ambassador has no authority to bind The Aquatic Coach.

**2.3 Non-exclusivity.** The Ambassador may promote other coaching or training services, provided such promotion does not conflict with Section 5 (Prohibited Conduct) and does not use Confidential Information.

**2.4 No minimum income guarantee.** The Aquatic Coach does not guarantee any minimum number of referrals, impressions, or Commission payments.

---

## 3. AMBASSADOR OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Active use.** The Ambassador shall remain an active, paying Platform subscriber throughout the Term, except during approved medical leave of up to sixty (60) consecutive days with prior notice.

**3.2 Posting habit.** The Ambassador shall maintain an average of at least **three (3) posts per calendar week** on their primary Social Media channel throughout the Term, consistent with the posting habit verified at application.

**3.3 Weekly Recommended Post.** The Ambassador shall publish at least **one (1) Recommended Post per calendar week** (Monday through Sunday) on at least one approved Social Media channel.

**3.4 Target Race narrative.** The Ambassador shall publicly document training toward the **Target Race** in Recommended Posts and in other program-related content. At least **seventy percent (70%)** of program-tagged Ambassador content during the Term shall relate to the Target Race and Primary Sport Lens declared in Schedule C.

**3.5 Content format mix.** Across all weekly Social Media content, the Ambassador shall maintain a mix of **Reels/short video and static photos or carousels**, including an average of at least **one (1) Reel or short video per week**. The Ambassador shall publish at least **one (1) Reel** using Photoshoot Deliverables within **thirty (30) days** of receipt.

**3.6 Referral tools.** The Ambassador shall use the assigned Referral Link and/or Referral Code in every Recommended Post.

**3.7 Disclosure.** The Ambassador shall include clear, conspicuous disclosure of the commercial relationship in every Recommended Post and any other post that mentions the Platform in exchange for potential Commission, in compliance with Section 5 and applicable law.

**3.8 Accuracy.** The Ambassador shall ensure all statements about the Platform are truthful, based on personal experience, and consistent with Brand Guidelines. The Ambassador shall not make guarantees regarding performance outcomes.

**3.9 Photoshoot.** The Aquatic Coach shall provide one (1) professional photoshoot session per Term, including still photography and short-form video clips suitable for Reels, as described in Schedule C. The Ambassador shall attend the photoshoot within **eight (8) weeks** of the Effective Date unless otherwise agreed in writing. Missed sessions without at least forty-eight (48) hours’ notice may be forfeited.

**3.10 Professional conduct.** The Ambassador shall not engage in conduct that could harm The Aquatic Coach’s reputation, including harassment, discrimination, doping promotion, or unsafe training advocacy.

**3.11 Reporting.** The Ambassador shall notify The Aquatic Coach within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of misinformation, technical issues affecting referrals, or compliance complaints related to their content.

**3.12 Cooperation.** The Ambassador shall reasonably cooperate with The Aquatic Coach’s quarterly program reviews and compliance checks.

---

## 4. CONTENT STANDARDS

**4.1 Ownership.** The Ambassador retains ownership of their original content and Photoshoot Deliverables created for the Ambassador. The Ambassador grants The Aquatic Coach a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to repost, share, or feature Ambassador content and Photoshoot Deliverables (with credit) for marketing purposes during and for twelve (12) months after the Term.

**4.2 Brand assets.** The Aquatic Coach grants the Ambassador a limited, revocable licence to use approved Brand Guidelines and logos solely for Recommended Posts during the Term.

**4.3 Recommended Post requirements.** Each Recommended Post must:

(a) be published during the Term;  
(b) include a personal account of training with the Platform **toward the Target Race**;  
(c) reference the **Target Race** or race-build progression (countdown, key session, taper, or reflection);  
(d) name “The Aquatic Coach Platform” or “The Aquatic Coach”;  
(e) include a call to action to sign up or join;  
(f) include the Referral Link or Referral Code;  
(g) include disclosure per Section 3.7;  
(h) include at least one original image, Reel, or video; and  
(i) remain publicly visible for at least thirty (30) days.

**4.4 Pre-approval.** During the first four (4) weeks of the Term, the Ambassador may (and if requested by The Aquatic Coach, shall) submit drafts to marketing@andyastfalck.com at least forty-eight (48) hours before publication.

**4.5 Takedown.** The Aquatic Coach may require modification or removal of content that violates this Agreement within forty-eight (48) hours of written notice. Failure to comply is a material breach.

---

## 5. PROHIBITED CONDUCT

The Ambassador shall not:

(a) make false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about the Platform;  
(b) guarantee specific race results, times, health outcomes, or injury prevention;  
(c) provide medical or nutrition advice on behalf of The Aquatic Coach;  
(d) use the Platform’s or other athletes’ private data in public content;  
(e) create fake accounts, reviews, or referral sign-ups;  
(f) refer themselves or members of their household;  
(g) run paid search advertising on The Aquatic Coach trademarks without written approval;  
(h) state or imply they are an employee or official spokesperson of The Aquatic Coach;  
(i) disparage competitors in a deceptive or unlawful manner; or  
(j) violate applicable advertising, consumer protection, or data protection laws.

---

## 6. REFERRAL COMMISSION

**6.1 Commission amount.** For each Referred Athlete, The Aquatic Coach shall pay the Ambassador **€100 (one hundred euros)**.

**6.2 Source.** Commission is funded from the First Month Subscription Fee paid by the Referred Athlete. Payment of Commission does not reduce or refund any amount owed by the Referred Athlete.

**6.3 Qualification conditions.** Commission is payable only if:

(a) attribution is recorded to a Recommended Post within the Attribution Window;  
(b) the Referred Athlete completes registration and pays the First Month Subscription Fee in full;  
(c) thirty (30) days pass without refund, chargeback, or payment reversal;  
(d) the sign-up is not fraudulent or duplicate, as determined by The Aquatic Coach in good faith; and  
(e) the Ambassador is in compliance with this Agreement at the time of payment.

**6.4 Caps.** Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

(a) maximum **€1,000** Commission per calendar month; and  
(b) maximum **€10,000** Commission per calendar year.

**6.5 Attribution priority.** If multiple Recommended Posts are clicked, the last qualifying click within the Attribution Window before registration receives attribution.

**6.6 Records.** The Aquatic Coach’s referral tracking system is the primary record for attribution. Disputes must be raised within fourteen (14) days of the Ambassador’s notification of a sign-up or non-attributed sign-up, with reasonable supporting evidence.

---

## 7. PAYMENT

**7.1 Schedule.** Approved Commissions are paid by the last business day of the month in which they are approved, provided they were confirmed before the fifteenth (15th) of that month.

**7.2 Method.** Payment via SEPA bank transfer or PayPal to the account designated by the Ambassador.

**7.3 Minimum payout.** If the approved balance is below €100, it rolls forward to the next payment period.

**7.4 Invoice.** Where required by law (including EU B2B services), the Ambassador shall issue a valid invoice before payment. The Aquatic Coach may operate self-billing if agreed in Schedule B.

**7.5 Taxes.** The Ambassador is solely responsible for all taxes, social contributions, and reporting obligations arising from Commission payments. The Aquatic Coach may withhold amounts if required by law.

**7.6 Currency.** All amounts are in euros (EUR).

---

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1** The Ambassador shall not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent.

**8.2** Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the Ambassador, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

**8.3** This obligation survives termination for two (2) years.

---

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

**9.1** Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law, including the GDPR.

**9.2** The Ambassador shall not share personal data of potential or actual Referred Athletes with The Aquatic Coach except through official referral mechanisms.

**9.3** Where the Ambassador processes personal data in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall execute a separate data processing agreement if required by law.

---

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

**10.1 Initial term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **six (6) months**.

**10.2 Renewal.** Automatically renews for successive six (6) month periods unless either Party gives **thirty (30) days’ written notice** before the end of the current period.

**10.3 Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate on thirty (30) days’ written notice.

**10.4 Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches and fails to cure within fourteen (14) days of notice (or immediately for fraud, illegal conduct, or reputational harm).

**10.5 Effect of termination.**

(a) Referral Links deactivate within fourteen (14) days;  
(b) pending qualified Commissions are paid per Section 7 if the Ambassador was in compliance when the Referred Athlete converted;  
(c) the Ambassador removes active ambassador badges and unpaid promotional claims within seven (7) days;  
(d) Sections 1, 4.1 (licence to The Aquatic Coach for prior content), 6.6, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 13 survive.

---

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**11.1** The Aquatic Coach retains all rights in the Platform, trademarks, protocols, and Brand Guidelines.

**11.2** The Ambassador shall not register domain names, social handles, or trademarks confusingly similar to The Aquatic Coach.

**11.3** Upon termination, the Ambassador’s licence to Brand Guidelines ends, except for archival posts that cannot reasonably be edited (historical disclosure must remain).

## 12. LIABILITY

**12.1** The Ambassador participates at their own risk in training and social media activities.

**12.2** To the maximum extent permitted by law, The Aquatic Coach’s total liability under this Agreement is limited to Commissions owed and unpaid in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**12.3** Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or lost-profit damages.

**12.4** The Ambassador indemnifies The Aquatic Coach against claims arising from the Ambassador’s content, unlawful conduct, or breach of this Agreement, except where caused by The Aquatic Coach’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct.
