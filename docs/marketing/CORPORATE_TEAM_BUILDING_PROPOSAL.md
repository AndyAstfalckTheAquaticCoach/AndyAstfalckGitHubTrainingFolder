## 1. Executive Summary

The Aquatic Coach Platform Corporate Team Building Partnership offers **medium-sized businesses complimentary access** to a structured, coach-led **team running program** designed to build fitness, morale, and shared achievement around a collective running goal — for example, a corporate 5K, 10K, half marathon, or relay event.

In return for this free program, the participating business agrees to promote the partnership through its **own corporate social media** and through **five selected team members** who post weekly from their personal accounts. The business may also supply **media assets from group training runs** for use in co-branded content.

This is a **value exchange partnership**, not a paid sponsorship. The business receives professional coaching infrastructure, team accountability, and event preparation at no subscription cost. The Aquatic Coach receives authentic workplace visibility, finish-line storytelling, and credible B2B brand exposure.

## 3. Who This Is For

### 3.1 Ideal business profile

This proposal targets **medium-sized businesses** that want a structured wellbeing initiative tied to a concrete team outcome.

| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Company size** | Typically **50–250 employees** (medium enterprise under EU SME framing); businesses outside this range may apply and will be assessed individually |
| **Location** | Netherlands or EU/EEA preferred; hybrid/remote teams accepted if a core group can train together periodically |
| **Commitment** | Minimum **5 employees** willing to participate as named team members and social advocates |
| **Marketing capacity** | Active corporate social media presence (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, or X) |
| **Leadership buy-in** | HR/People, Marketing, or leadership sponsor willing to approve social posting and employee participation |
| **Event horizon** | A target team running goal within **12–20 weeks** of program start (e.g., corporate race, charity run, city 10K) |

### 3.2 Suitable team running goals

Examples of approved team goals:

- Complete a named **5K / 10K / half marathon** as a corporate team
- Achieve a **collective distance target** over the program (e.g., 1,000 km as a team)
- Enter a **relay or multi-leg corporate challenge**
- Support a **charity run** with a branded company squad

The Aquatic Coach will confirm goal feasibility based on participant baseline fitness and timeline.

## 5. Platform Overview — What the Team Experiences

Participants train on the same evidence-based system used by individual athletes on The Aquatic Coach Platform:

1. **Structured running progression** — Weekly sessions building toward the team event, with appropriate recovery and load management
2. **Intervals.icu integration** — Training data interpreted consistently; participants see how metrics inform session decisions
3. **Individual readiness within a team frame** — Team goal does not override safety; overloaded participants receive modified sessions
4. **Pre- and post-session reporting** — Briefings and feedback that keep the squad aligned and accountable
5. **Race-week intelligence** — Taper targets, pacing guidance, and go/no-go readiness for race day
6. **Group training compatibility** — Sessions designed so mixed-ability groups can train together where appropriate

The business should describe these benefits honestly based on the team’s lived experience — not as guaranteed performance outcomes.

## 6. What the Business Provides in Return

The partnership is contingent on the business fulfilling its **social media and content obligations**. These are the consideration for the free program.

### 6.1 Overview of posting obligations

| Channel | Who posts | Frequency | Content type |
|---------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Corporate social media** | Business official account(s) | **1 post per week** | Team training update, program milestone, or co-branded group run content |
| **Employee social media** | **5 selected team members** | **1 post per week each** (5 posts/week total) | Personal training experience + mention of The Aquatic Coach Platform |
| **Race day** | Corporate account + 5 selected team members | **1 pre-race post each** (6 posts on race day) | Pre-race excitement, team readiness, platform mention |
| **Post-race** | Corporate account + 5 selected team members | **1 finish-line post each** (6 posts within 48 hours) | Finish-line photo/video + platform mention |

**Total minimum posts during a 12-week block:**

- Weekly posts: 12 weeks × 6 posts = **72 posts**
- Race day pre-race: **6 posts**
- Post-race finish line: **6 posts**
- **Grand total: 84 posts** over the partnership term (excluding optional bonus content)

### 6.2 Selected team members (the “Platform 5”)

The business nominates **5 employees** who:

- Are active participants in the team running program
- Maintain a public personal social media account (LinkedIn, Instagram, Strava, Facebook, or X)
- Commit to weekly posting for the full partnership term
- Appear in at least **2 group training runs** during the program (for media asset creation)
- Post pre-race and finish-line content on race day / immediately after

Substitutions require written approval from The Aquatic Coach if a nominated member leaves the company or cannot continue posting.

### 6.3 Corporate social media posts

The business’s official account(s) must:

- Publish **one post per calendar week** (Monday–Sunday) referencing the team program and The Aquatic Coach Platform
- Include the partnership disclosure (Section 8)
- Tag The Aquatic Coach where the platform supports tagging (@AndyAstfalck or official handle as confirmed at onboarding)
- Publish **pre-race posts on race day** and **finish-line posts within 48 hours** of the event

Corporate posts may be drafted by Marketing/HR with content prompts supplied by The Aquatic Coach.

### 6.4 Weekly employee posts — expectations

Each of the 5 selected team members publishes **one post per week** on their personal account covering:

1. **Personal training update** — What they did that week in the program (session, milestone, challenge, or recovery)
2. **Platform mention** — Clear reference to training with **The Aquatic Coach Platform**
3. **Team context** — Reference to the corporate team goal or group training where appropriate
4. **Disclosure** — Partnership disclosure (Section 8)
5. **Visual** — At least one original photo or short video (training run, group session, data screenshot, or team moment)

Recommended mix across the 5 members: rotate themes (readiness, group run, long run, recovery, race prep) so the team’s feed is varied, not repetitive.

### 6.5 Race day — pre-race posts

On **race day**, before the start:

| Poster | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **Corporate account** | Team photo or graphic; good luck message; event name; mention of coaching via The Aquatic Coach Platform |
| **Each of the 5 team members** | Personal pre-race post (excitement, goal, gratitude to team/coach); platform mention; disclosure |

Pre-race posts should go live **before the race start time** where possible.

### 6.6 Post-race — finish-line posts

Within **48 hours** of finishing:

| Poster | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **Corporate account** | Finish-line team shot or collage; celebration of team achievement; thank you to The Aquatic Coach Platform |
| **Each of the 5 team members** | Personal finish-line photo or medal/bib shot; reflection on the program; platform mention; disclosure |

Finish-line posts are **mandatory** partnership deliverables and carry the highest visibility value for both parties.

## 7. Media Assets from Group Training Runs

### 7.1 Purpose

Group training runs create authentic visual content for corporate and employee posts. The business may **provide media assets** captured during organised team sessions for use in the partnership.

### 7.2 Asset types accepted

| Asset type | Examples |
|------------|----------|
| **Photography** | Group start-line, mid-run, post-run team photo, branded kit if worn |
| **Short video** | 15–60 second clips of team running, cheering, or cooldown |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | Warm-up, team briefing, hydration stop, post-run stretch |
| **Optional drone / wide shots** | Subject to venue permissions and safety rules |

### 7.3 Who provides assets

- **Business-provided:** Marketing/HR or a designated employee photographer supplies raw files via shared folder (Google Drive, Dropbox, or equivalent)
- **The Aquatic Coach–provided:** Where on-site or remote coach attendance occurs, The Aquatic Coach may supply additional assets for co-use
- **Employee-generated:** Platform 5 members may use their own training and finish-line photos in personal posts

### 7.4 Usage rights

By providing assets, the business grants The Aquatic Coach a **non-exclusive licence** to use supplied media (with credit to the company) for:

- Reposting on The Aquatic Coach social channels
- Case study, website, and partnership marketing
- Program promotion to other businesses

The business retains ownership of its logos and brand marks. The Aquatic Coach retains ownership of platform branding. Co-branded templates will be supplied at onboarding.

### 7.5 Minimum asset contribution

The business should supply media from at least **2 group training runs** during the program (minimum **10 usable images** or **2 short video clips** total). Higher contribution is encouraged and may be featured prominently on The Aquatic Coach channels.

## 8. Disclosure, Compliance & Brand Standards

### 8.1 Mandatory disclosure

All partnership posts (corporate and employee) must include a clear disclosure, such as:

- *“Our team is training free of charge with The Aquatic Coach Platform in exchange for sharing our journey on social media.”*
- *“#Partnership #TeamBuild #AquaticCoach”* (must accompany written disclosure, not replace it)

Employee posts must clarify the **value exchange** — complimentary program access in return for social content.

### 8.2 Platform mentions

Acceptable mention examples:

- “Training for our company 10K with The Aquatic Coach Platform”
- “Our team’s running program is powered by The Aquatic Coach Platform”
- “Readiness-led coaching via The Aquatic Coach Platform”

Avoid:

- Claims that the platform guarantees race times or injury prevention
- Medical or occupational health claims without appropriate review
- Statements that imply endorsement by the race organiser unless true

### 8.3 Brand approval

- Corporate posts: business retains editorial control; The Aquatic Coach may request factual corrections within 48 hours
- Employee posts: authentic personal voice encouraged; must remain accurate and professional
- The Aquatic Coach supplies a **logo pack, tag handles, and 3 co-branded post templates** at onboarding

- Deliver complimentary team running program for agreed participant count and term
- Provide onboarding, content prompts, and co-branded templates
- Monitor training load and readiness at team level
- Supply race-week and pacing guidance

### 10.3 Business Marketing / HR contact

- Publish weekly corporate post (or delegate and verify)
- Maintain shared media folder for group run assets
- Remind Platform 5 of weekly posting obligations
- Escalate member substitutions or compliance issues

### 10.4 Platform 5 (selected team members)

- Complete weekly personal post with platform mention and disclosure
- Publish pre-race and finish-line posts
- Attend minimum 2 group training runs for media opportunities
- Engage authentically with comments on their posts

### 10.5 Wider participating employees (up to 15)

- Follow the training program on platform
- May appear in group photos and corporate posts
- Not subject to weekly personal posting unless added to Platform 5

## 11. Compliance Monitoring & Consequences

### 11.1 Tracking

The Aquatic Coach will maintain a simple **Partnership Post Log**:

- Weekly checklist: 1 corporate + 5 employee posts
- Race day checklist: 6 pre-race + 6 finish-line posts
- Media asset delivery log

The business contact receives a **fortnightly summary** during the program.

### 11.2 Missed posts

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **1 missed post in a week** (any channel) | Friendly reminder; catch-up within 7 days |
| **2+ missed posts in a rolling 4-week period** | Written notice to business sponsor; corrective plan |
| **Failure to publish race day or finish-line posts** | Material breach — may trigger early termination or invoice for program value |
| **Persistent non-compliance (>3 weeks cumulative misses)** | The Aquatic Coach may terminate complimentary access |

### 11.3 Good standing

Partners in good standing may be invited to:

- Renew for a second event season
- Receive extended participant caps
- Be featured as a flagship case study on The Aquatic Coach website
