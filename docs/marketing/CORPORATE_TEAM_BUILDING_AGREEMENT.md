## RECITALS

**WHEREAS** The Aquatic Coach operates The Aquatic Coach Platform, providing evidence-based endurance coaching and team training programs;

**WHEREAS** the Company wishes to offer its employees a structured team running program for team building and a collective running goal at no platform subscription cost;

**WHEREAS** in exchange for complimentary program access, the Company agrees to publish social media content from its corporate accounts and through five selected employee participants;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** **“Agreement”** means this Corporate Team Building Partnership Agreement and its schedules.

**1.2** **“Company Media”** means photographs, videos, and other assets captured during Group Training Runs and supplied by the Company to The Aquatic Coach.

**1.3** **“Corporate Account”** means the Company’s official social media account(s) listed in Schedule A.

**1.4** **“Finish-Line Posts”** means post-race social media posts including a finish-line image or equivalent and a mention of The Aquatic Coach Platform, due within forty-eight (48) hours of the Event finish.

**1.5** **“Group Training Run”** means an organised team running session involving multiple Participants.

**1.6** **“Event”** means the target team running goal specified in Schedule A (race, relay, or collective challenge).

**1.7** **“Event Date”** means the date of the Event.

**1.8** **“Partnership Term”** means the period from the Kickoff Date through seven (7) days after the Event Date.

**1.9** **“Platform”** means The Aquatic Coach Platform and related coaching services.

**1.10** **“Platform 5”** means the five (5) employees designated in Schedule B who publish weekly personal social media posts.

**1.11** **“Pre-Race Posts”** means social media posts published on the Event Date before the Event start time (or within two (2) hours before start where timezone/posting constraints apply).

**1.12** **“Program”** means the complimentary team running program provided under Section 3.

**1.13** **“Weekly Posts”** means one (1) Corporate Account post and five (5) Platform 5 personal posts per calendar week (Monday–Sunday).

---

## 2. PARTNERSHIP MODEL

**2.1 Value exchange.** The Program is provided **free of charge** as consideration for the Company’s fulfilment of social media, media asset, and disclosure obligations in this Agreement.

**2.2 Non-sponsorship.** This is not a paid sponsorship. Unless separately agreed in writing, The Aquatic Coach does not pay fees to the Company.

**2.3 Independent relationship.** The Company participates voluntarily. Nothing herein creates employment, agency, or joint venture between the Parties.

---

## 3. COMPLIMENTARY PROGRAM (THE AQUATIC COACH OBLIGATIONS)

**3.1 Scope.** The Aquatic Coach shall provide the Program to up to **fifteen (15) Participants** for the Partnership Term, comprising:

(a) structured team running programming aligned to the Event;  
(b) Platform access and onboarding for enrolled Participants;  
(c) readiness-aware load management and race-week guidance;  
(d) programming support for up to **two (2) Group Training Runs per month** (remote coaching; on-site subject to separate agreement);  
(e) co-branded content templates and weekly posting prompts;  
(f) a Partnership contact for the duration of the Term.

**3.2 Estimated commercial value.** The complimentary package has an estimated value of **€[PACKAGE_VALUE]** for internal reference and breach calculation (Section 9).

**3.3 Standard of service.** The Aquatic Coach will deliver the Program with reasonable skill and care consistent with its standard coaching practice. The Aquatic Coach does not guarantee Event completion times, injury avoidance, or specific performance outcomes.

**3.4 Kickoff.** Program delivery begins on the **Kickoff Date** in Schedule A, subject to Participant onboarding completion.

---

## 4. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS — SOCIAL MEDIA

**4.1 Weekly Posts.** The Company shall ensure publication of **Weekly Posts** commencing no later than **Week 2** of the Partnership Term and continuing through Event Date:

| Obligation | Minimum |
|------------|---------|
| Corporate Account | 1 post per calendar week |
| Each Platform 5 member | 1 personal post per calendar week |
| **Total** | **6 posts per week** |

**4.2 Content requirements.** Each Weekly Post, Pre-Race Post, and Finish-Line Post must:

(a) reference the Company’s team running program;  
(b) mention **The Aquatic Coach Platform** by name at least once;  
(c) include partnership disclosure per Section 6;  
(d) include at least one original image or video (except Corporate Account text-only reposts of Company Media are permitted if image is Company-owned);  
(e) remain publicly visible for at least thirty (30) days.

**4.3 Pre-Race Posts.** On the Event Date, the Company shall ensure:

(a) **one (1) Pre-Race Post** on the Corporate Account; and  
(b) **one (1) Pre-Race Post** from each Platform 5 member on their personal account.

**4.4 Finish-Line Posts.** Within **forty-eight (48) hours** of the Event finish, the Company shall ensure:

(a) **one (1) Finish-Line Post** on the Corporate Account featuring a team or representative finish-line image; and  
(b) **one (1) Finish-Line Post** from each Platform 5 member including a finish-line photo or personal finish image and Platform mention.

**4.5 Posting log.** The Company’s Partnership contact shall maintain a simple log of published posts and share it fortnightly upon request.

**4.6 Substitutions.** Changes to Platform 5 members require prior written approval from The Aquatic Coach and updated Schedule B.

---

## 5. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS — MEDIA ASSETS

**5.1 Supply.** The Company shall provide Company Media from at least **two (2) Group Training Runs** during the Partnership Term, comprising at minimum:

(a) **ten (10) usable photographs**, or  
(b) **two (2) video clips** of fifteen (15) seconds or longer, or  
(c) an equivalent combination.

**5.2 Delivery.** Assets shall be uploaded to a shared folder designated at Kickoff within **seven (7) days** of each Group Training Run.

**5.3 Licence to The Aquatic Coach.** The Company grants The Aquatic Coach a **non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence** to use Company Media (with Company name credit) for reposting, case studies, website features, and promotion of the corporate partnership program during and for **twenty-four (24) months** after the Partnership Term.

**5.4 Company marks.** The Aquatic Coach shall not alter Company logos except for sizing/cropping necessary for social formats.

**5.5 Participant consent.** The Company confirms it has obtained necessary consent from employees appearing in Company Media for the uses described herein.

---

## 6. DISCLOSURE & COMPLIANCE

**6.1 Disclosure text.** All partnership posts must include a clear value-exchange disclosure, e.g.:

*“Our team receives complimentary access to The Aquatic Coach Platform in exchange for sharing our training journey on social media.”*

**6.2 Employee posts.** Platform 5 personal posts must include equivalent disclosure in the first three lines or as on-screen text in video.

**6.3 Accuracy.** The Company shall not make false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about the Platform, including guaranteed results or medical outcomes.

**6.4 Laws.** The Company shall comply with applicable advertising, consumer protection, employment, and data protection laws (including GDPR) in all partnership content and participant data handling.

**6.5 Internal policies.** The Company is responsible for ensuring employee posting complies with its own social media and HR policies.

---

## 7. PLATFORM 5 & PARTICIPANTS

**7.1 Nomination.** The Company shall nominate Platform 5 members in Schedule B before Kickoff. Each shall:

(a) be an active Participant in the Program;  
(b) maintain a public social media account;  
(c) attend at least **two (2) Group Training Runs**;  
(d) comply with posting obligations personally or via pre-approved delegate (delegate use requires The Aquatic Coach consent).

**7.2 Wider participants.** Up to fifteen (15) total Participants may access the Program. Non–Platform 5 Participants are not subject to weekly personal posting requirements.

**7.3 Conduct.** The Company shall ensure Participants do not engage in conduct that materially harms The Aquatic Coach’s reputation in connection with the Program.

---

## 8. BRAND & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Aquatic Coach IP.** The Aquatic Coach retains all rights in the Platform, trademarks, protocols, and coaching materials.

**8.2 Company IP.** The Company retains all rights in its name, logos, and Company Media.

**8.3 Co-branded materials.** Templates supplied by The Aquatic Coach may be used by the Company solely for this partnership during the Term.

**8.4 No endorsement.** Neither Party may state or imply the other endorses unrelated products or services without written consent.

---

## 9. MONITORING, REMEDIES & BREACH

**9.1 Monitoring.** The Aquatic Coach may track public posts and asset delivery against this Agreement.

**9.2 Cure period.** If the Company misses Weekly Posts, Pre-Race Posts, Finish-Line Posts, or media minimums, The Aquatic Coach will notify the Partnership contact. The Company has **seven (7) days** to cure unless Event Date timing makes cure impossible.

**9.3 Material breach.** The following are material breaches:

(a) failure to publish Pre-Race Posts or Finish-Line Posts in full (6 + 6);  
(b) fewer than **four (4) of six (6)** Weekly Posts in any two (2) consecutive weeks after cure notice;  
(c) failure to supply minimum Company Media by Event Date;  
(d) fraudulent or undisclosed paid promotion presenting the Program as unsolicited endorsement;  
(e) use of the Platform or partnership in a unlawful or reputational harmful manner.

**9.4 Remedies.** On material breach uncured within the cure period, The Aquatic Coach may:

(a) suspend Platform access for non-participating employees while maintaining access for compliant Platform 5;  
(b) terminate this Agreement immediately; and/or  
(c) invoice the Company for **€[PACKAGE_VALUE]** (or pro-rata portion for partial Term fulfilment) representing the complimentary Program value, payable within thirty (30) days.

**9.5 Good faith.** Both Parties will act in good faith to resolve posting shortfalls before exercising termination or invoicing.

**11.3** Survival: two (2) years after termination.

## 12. TERM & TERMINATION

**12.1 Term.** Begins on the Effective Date and ends seven (7) days after the Event Date unless terminated earlier.

**12.2 Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate with **fourteen (14) days’ written notice** if the Event is cancelled or postponed beyond ninety (90) days.

**12.3 Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately on uncured material breach per Section 9.

**12.4 Effect of termination.**

(a) Platform access ends for all Participants within seven (7) days;  
(b) Company Media licence to The Aquatic Coach survives per Section 5.3;  
(c) invoicing rights under Section 9.4 survive if applicable;  
(d) Finish-Line Posts already published may remain live with disclosure intact.

**13.2** To the maximum extent permitted by law, The Aquatic Coach’s total liability under this Agreement is limited to **€[PACKAGE_VALUE]** or the direct damages proven, whichever is lower.

**13.3** Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

**13.4** The Company indemnifies The Aquatic Coach against claims arising from Company Media, employee posts, or Company breach of this Agreement, except where caused by The Aquatic Coach’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct.
