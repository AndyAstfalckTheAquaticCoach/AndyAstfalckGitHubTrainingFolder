## 1. Executive Summary

The Aquatic Coach Platform Ambassador Program is a structured referral and advocacy initiative for athletes who already train with the platform. Ambassadors share authentic, weekly social media content about their experience with evidence-based, data-integrated coaching. When a post they recommend directly leads to a new athlete signing up and paying their first month subscription, the ambassador receives **€100** from that first month fee.

This program is designed to grow the platform through credible peer advocacy — not generic advertising. Ambassadors are **race-focused, multi-sport athletes** — predominantly women — who already publish regularly and document their training journey toward a **named target event**. The Aquatic Coach provides each ambassador with a **professional photoshoot including video assets** for Reels, alongside referral commission when recommended posts convert.

## 3. Platform Overview (What Ambassadors Promote)

The Aquatic Coach Platform combines **on-site aquatic technical coaching** with **remote, data-integrated performance management** built on Intervals.icu and the Section 11 AI Coach Protocol.

### Core benefits ambassadors may reference

1. **Evidence-based coaching** — Recommendations grounded in validated endurance science (load management, periodisation, readiness, nutrition, race preparation), not generic training advice.
2. **Deterministic readiness decisions** — Daily go / modify / skip guidance based on HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, training load, and recovery signals.
3. **Intervals.icu integration** — Training data mirrored and interpreted consistently; athletes see how their metrics inform session decisions.
4. **Multi-sport support** — Swimming, cycling, running, triathlon, open-water, and strength training with per-sport threshold isolation.
5. **Structured reporting** — Pre-workout briefings, post-workout analysis, and block reports that athletes can act on immediately.
6. **Hybrid coaching model** — Remote programming plus optional on-site swim biomechanics and efficiency work (where geographically available).
7. **Race-ready intelligence** — Taper management, sustainability profiling, course-specific pacing, and environmental condition adjustments.
8. **Safety-first approach** — Acute overload and recovery signals trigger modification or rest before performance is compromised.

Ambassadors must describe benefits accurately and from personal experience. They must not invent metrics, guarantees, or outcomes the platform has not delivered for them.

## 4. Ideal Ambassador Profile & Cohort Design

### 4.1 Who we are looking for

Ambassadors are **creators who already train in public**. They mix sports in real life (e.g. swim + run, triathlon, run + strength) but each ambassador is recruited with **one primary race target** and **one lead sport narrative** for the program term.

| Cohort design rule | Target |
|--------------------|--------|
| **Gender balance** | **Majority women** — aim for **≥60% women** in each intake cohort (remaining places open to all genders) |
| **Follower minimum** | **More than 3,000** followers/subscribers on primary channel (Instagram or TikTok preferred; YouTube/Strava considered case-by-case) |
| **Pre-program posting** | Already posts **at least 3 times per week** (any mix of content) for minimum **8 of the prior 12 weeks** — verified at application |
| **Race focus** | Must be training on the Platform toward a **named target race** within the ambassador term (see Section 4.3) |
| **Content format habit** | Already uses a **mix of Reels/video and static photos** — not photo-only or text-only accounts |
| **Platform use** | Active Aquatic Coach Platform subscriber using prescribed training toward that race |

### 4.2 Multi-sport athletes — one primary focus each

We welcome athletes who **cross-train**, but each ambassador slot is defined by a **single race story** and **primary sport lens**:

| Ambassador type | Primary sport lens | Typical mixed training | Example target races |
|-----------------|-------------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **Open-water swimmer** | Swim | Pool technique, light run for fitness | 1 km / 3 km OW, charity swim |
| **Triathlete** | Triathlon (swim-led narrative OK) | Bike, run, swim | Sprint/Olympic tri, relay |
| **Runner** | Run | Strength, occasional cross-train | 5 km, 10 km, 15 km business run |
| **Swim-run / hybrid** | Swim or run (declared at signup) | Second sport supporting race | OW + trail, aquathlon prep |
| **Pool swimmer (masters)** | Swim | Dryland, mobility | Masters meet, 1500m focus |

**Rule:** Cross-training may appear in posts, but **≥70% of program-tagged ambassador content** must relate to the declared **Target Race** and **primary sport lens**.

### 4.3 Target race requirement

Every ambassador must declare at application:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Target Race name** | Official event name (e.g. “ZOZ Duckrace 1 km”, “NN CPC Loop Den Haag 10 km Business”) |
| **Race date** | Within **6 months** of ambassador start (extendable by agreement) |
| **Race distance** | Aligned to training plan on Platform |
| **Narrative commitment** | Publicly document **training for this race** in weekly ambassador posts |

Posts that only discuss generic fitness **without race progression** do not meet program standards. Recommended posts should reference race countdown, key sessions, taper, or lessons learned in build phases.

**Example narrative arc:**  
*Week 1–4 base → Week 5–8 build → Week 9–11 race-specific → Week 12 taper/race → Post-race reflection*

### 4.4 Social content standards (pre-existing habit)

Applicants must already demonstrate:

| Metric | Minimum |
|--------|---------|
| **Posting frequency** | **≥3 posts per week** average (all content types) |
| **Format mix (prior 4 weeks)** | At least **40% video/Reels** and **40% photos/carousel** (remaining may be either) |
| **Primary platform** | Instagram or TikTok strongly preferred for Reels workflow |
| **Engagement** | Authentic audience — no purchased followers; The Aquatic Coach may request insights screenshot |

During the program, ambassadors maintain their natural **≥3 posts/week** rhythm. Of those, **at least 1 per week** is the mandatory **Recommended Post** (Section 7) with referral link and disclosure. Additional posts should continue the **race-training narrative** even when not commission-tagged.

### 4.5 Ambassador photoshoot benefit

Each approved ambassador receives **one professional photoshoot session** per 6-month term, including:

| Included | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Still photography** | Training-themed images (pool, open water, run, bike, or gym — aligned to primary sport) |
| **Video clips for Reels** | Short vertical clips (minimum **5 usable clips**, 15–60 seconds) suitable for Instagram/TikTok Reels |
| **Co-branding** | Subtle Aquatic Coach / platform branding where agreed (e.g. kit, cap, overlay) |
| **Usage rights** | Ambassador receives licence to use all assets for personal social media; The Aquatic Coach receives licence to repost with credit |
| **Timing** | Scheduled within **first 8 weeks** of term, ideally **before Target Race** so assets fuel race-build content |
| **Location** | Netherlands / Randstad where possible; travel beyond agreed radius at ambassador’s cost unless otherwise agreed |

The photoshoot is a **program benefit**, not payment in lieu of Commission. Missed sessions without 48-hour notice may be forfeited.

### 4.6 Cohort composition (pilot intake example)

For a first cohort of **10 ambassadors**:

| Slot | Profile example |
|------|-----------------|
| 4 | Women — open-water / swim → OW race |
| 3 | Women — run or tri → 5k–10k or sprint tri |
| 1 | Woman — hybrid swim-run |
| 2 | Open — any gender, any eligible primary lens |

Adjust per intake while keeping **women ≥60%**.

## 6. Program Structure

### 6.1 Ambassador role

Ambassadors are independent advocates — not employees, agents, or partners of The Aquatic Coach unless separately contracted. Their role is to:

- Share weekly content about their training experience on the platform
- Recommend the platform when appropriate to their audience
- Use assigned referral links or codes so sign-ups can be attributed
- Respond professionally to questions they choose to engage with
- Report concerns or inaccurate public comments about the platform to The Aquatic Coach

### 6.2 Referral commission

When an ambassador’s **recommended post** (see Section 8) **directly leads** to a new athlete:

1. Clicking the ambassador’s unique referral link or using their referral code, **and**
2. Completing registration on The Aquatic Coach Platform, **and**
3. Paying the full **first month subscription fee** without refund or chargeback within 30 days,

the ambassador receives a **€100 referral commission**, paid from the first month fee collected.

| Element | Terms |
|---------|-------|
| **Commission amount** | €100 (fixed per qualified referral) |
| **Source** | First month subscription fee of the referred athlete |
| **Maximum per referral** | One commission per referred athlete (no repeat payments for the same person) |
| **Monthly cap** | €1,000 per ambassador per calendar month (10 qualified referrals) unless otherwise agreed in writing |
| **Annual cap** | €10,000 per ambassador per calendar year unless otherwise agreed in writing |

**Note:** The standard first month subscription fee is defined in the Ambassador Agreement. Commission is payable only after The Aquatic Coach confirms payment clearance and attribution (Section 9).

## 7. Weekly Social Media Posting — Expectations

### 7.1 Two layers of posting

| Layer | Who | Requirement |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **A. Existing habit** | Pre-qualification | **≥3 posts per week** on primary channel (verified at application) |
| **B. Program obligations** | During term | **1 Recommended Post per week** + maintain Reels/photo mix and race narrative |

Ambassadors keep their natural **≥3 posts/week** rhythm throughout the term. Only one of those posts each week must be the commission-eligible **Recommended Post**; the other posts should continue the **Target Race training story** even when not referral-tagged.

### 7.2 Frequency and timing (Recommended Post)

| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| **Minimum frequency** | 1 Recommended Post per calendar week (Monday–Sunday) |
| **Recommended day** | Same day each week where possible (consistency builds audience expectation) |
| **Missed week policy** | One missed week per calendar quarter permitted without penalty; second miss triggers written reminder; third miss in a rolling 12-week period may result in program suspension |
| **Programme start** | Posting obligations begin 7 days after Ambassador Agreement is signed |

### 7.3 Format mix — Reels and photos

Across **all weekly content** (not only the Recommended Post), ambassadors maintain the mix they demonstrated at application:

| Format | Target |
|--------|--------|
| **Reels / short video** | **≥1 per week** on average |
| **Photos / carousels** | Regular presence — not a video-only account |
| **Photoshoot assets** | At least **1 Reel from the ambassador photoshoot** published within 30 days of delivery |

### 7.4 Required elements (Recommended Post)

Each weekly Recommended Post must include:

1. **Personal experience** — A specific, honest reference to training with The Aquatic Coach Platform toward the **Target Race** (e.g., readiness decision, session outcome, report insight, technique focus, race countdown)
2. **Race narrative** — Reference to **Target Race** progression (countdown, key session, build phase, taper, or post-race reflection)
3. **Platform mention** — Clear reference to “The Aquatic Coach Platform” or “The Aquatic Coach” (at least once)
4. **Referral mechanism** — Ambassador’s unique referral link or code in the post, bio link, or pinned comment (platform-dependent)
5. **Disclosure** — Mandatory partnership disclosure (Section 10)
6. **Visual or media** — At least one original image, Reel, or carousel frame (no stock imagery presented as the ambassador’s training)

### 7.5 Recommended content themes (rotate across weeks)

Ambassadors should vary content to avoid repetition. Suggested themes:

| Week theme | Format | Example angle |
|------------|--------|---------------|
| **Race build** | Reel or carousel | “Week 6 of my [Target Race] plan — today’s threshold session from Aquatic Coach” |
| **Readiness & recovery** | Reel or photo | How a go/modify/skip decision affected your race prep week |
| **Data-informed training** | Reel | POV clip + app screen or voiceover on Intervals.icu-informed session |
| **Swim / technique** | Photo or Reel | Pool or open-water focus tied to platform coaching for Target Race |
| **Photoshoot** | Reel (priority) | Branded clips from ambassador photoshoot session |
| **Load management** | Carousel | How structured periodisation is managing fatigue before Target Race |
| **Honest challenge** | Reel or photo | A setback, modification, or learning moment (builds credibility) |
| **Community / recommendation** | Any | Who the platform is best suited for and why you recommend it for race prep |

### 7.6 Content quality standards

Posts should be:

- **Authentic** — Written in the ambassador’s own voice, based on real training
- **Accurate** — No fabricated data, results, or platform capabilities
- **Concise** — Primary caption 150–400 words (platform-dependent; Reels/TikTok may be shorter with disclosure in caption or overlay)
- **Professional** — Free of offensive language, harassment, or discriminatory content
- **Evidence-aligned** — Consistent with The Aquatic Coach’s science-based positioning (avoid “miracle” or “guaranteed PR” language)

### 7.7 Pre-approval (optional but recommended)

Ambassadors may submit draft posts to marketing@andyastfalck.com for review **48 hours before publication** during their first 4 weeks in the program. After the onboarding period, pre-approval is optional unless a compliance issue has been flagged.

## 8. What Counts as a “Recommended Post”

A post qualifies as a **recommended post** for attribution purposes when **all** of the following are true:

1. It was published during the ambassador’s active program period
2. It includes the ambassador’s referral link or code
3. It contains an explicit recommendation to sign up, try, or join The Aquatic Coach Platform
4. It includes the required partnership disclosure
5. It remains publicly visible for a minimum of 30 days from publication
6. The referred athlete clicked that specific post’s referral link (or entered the code displayed in that post) **within 14 days** of the post going live

If multiple ambassador posts contributed to a sign-up, attribution goes to the **last qualifying recommended post** clicked before registration, within the 14-day attribution window.

Posts that mention the platform casually without a call-to-action or referral link **do not** qualify for commission attribution.

## 9. Referral Tracking & Attribution

### 9.1 Referral tools

Each ambassador receives:

- A unique **referral URL** (e.g., `https://aquaticcoach.com/join?ref=AMB-XXXX`)
- A matching **referral code** (e.g., `AMB-XXXX`)
- Access to an **Ambassador Dashboard** showing clicks, sign-ups, pending commissions, and paid commissions

### 9.2 Attribution rules

| Scenario | Commission payable? |
|----------|----------------------|
| User clicks referral link in qualifying recommended post → signs up → pays first month | **Yes** |
| User uses referral code from qualifying post at checkout | **Yes** |
| User signs up without referral link/code | **No** |
| User clicks link but signs up after 14-day window | **No** |
| User registers but cancels or receives full refund before day 30 | **No** |
| Referred user was already in The Aquatic Coach CRM or trial within prior 90 days | **No** |
| Ambassador refers themselves or a household member | **No** |
| Fraudulent, duplicate, or bot-generated sign-ups | **No** |

The Aquatic Coach’s attribution records are final unless the ambassador provides evidence of a tracking error within 14 days of the disputed event.

## 10. Disclosure, Compliance & Limits

### 10.1 Mandatory disclosure

All ambassador content must include a clear partnership disclosure. Acceptable examples:

- “I’m an ambassador for The Aquatic Coach Platform and may earn a referral fee if you sign up.”
- “Paid partnership / affiliate — The Aquatic Coach Platform.”
- “#ad #AquaticCoachAmbassador” (must appear alongside written disclosure on Instagram/Facebook)

Disclosure must appear **before** the “more” fold where applicable, or within the first 3 lines of the caption, or as an on-screen overlay in video content for the first 3 seconds.

### 10.2 Prohibited content and conduct

Ambassadors must **not**:

- Make medical claims or suggest the platform replaces medical advice
- Guarantee specific race times, weight loss, or injury outcomes
- Compare competitors by name in a disparaging way
- Use copyrighted music, images, or footage without licence
- Post content while under the influence or promoting unsafe training practices
- Create fake reviews or incentivise sign-ups without disclosure
- Bid on The Aquatic Coach branded search terms (paid ads)
- Impersonate The Aquatic Coach staff or speak as an official company representative
- Share confidential platform data, other athletes’ information, or unreleased features

### 10.3 Platform limits

| Limit | Policy |
|-------|--------|
| **Posts per week** | Minimum **3 total** (existing habit) including **1 Recommended Post**; maximum 3 program-tagged posts (additional organic mentions welcome but only qualifying recommended posts count for attribution) |
| **Cross-posting** | Same weekly post may be adapted across up to 2 platforms (e.g., Instagram + Strava) |
| **Boosted/paid posts** | Permitted only with prior written approval and compliant disclosure |
| **Giveaways tied to sign-up** | Require pre-approval; must comply with local competition law |
| **Commission stacking** | Referral commission cannot be combined with other unpublished discounts unless approved |

### 10.4 Regulatory compliance

Ambassadors are responsible for complying with applicable advertising and consumer protection laws in their jurisdiction, including:

- **EU:** Unfair Commercial Practices Directive; national advertising standards
- **Netherlands:** Reclame Code Commissie / Social Code: YouTube and influencer guidelines
- **UK (if applicable):** ASA CAP Code
- **General:** GDPR — no sharing of personal data of referred athletes

The Aquatic Coach may update disclosure templates when regulations change.

## 12. Onboarding Process

1. **Application** — Form captures: name, primary sport, **Target Race** (name + date), secondary sports, social handles, **follower count**, posting frequency self-assessment, sample post links, gender (optional — for cohort balance)
2. **Profile review** — Verify **3,000+ followers**, **≥3 posts/week** habit, Reels/photo mix, authentic race-prep narrative
3. **Cohort fit** — Confirm gender balance target (≥60% women) and sport/race diversity for upcoming intake
4. **Agreement** — Sign Ambassador Agreement including **Schedule C: Target Race & Profile**
5. **Setup** — Referral link, dashboard access, brand asset pack, disclosure templates
6. **Orientation** — 30-minute onboarding call covering content guidelines, attribution, **photoshoot scheduling**, and support contacts
7. **First post** — Race-focused Recommended Post published within 7 days of onboarding completion
8. **Photoshoot** — Delivered within first 8 weeks; ambassador publishes at least one Reel from session within 30 days

## 13. Performance Review & Program Continuation

The Aquatic Coach will conduct a lightweight quarterly review:

- Posting consistency (≥3/week overall + weekly Recommended Post compliance)
- Reels vs. photo mix maintained on profile
- Target Race narrative in content
- Photoshoot Reels published within 30 days of delivery
- Content quality and brand alignment
- Referral activity and conversion
- Compliance with disclosure requirements

Ambassadors who consistently meet expectations may be invited to extended terms, featured on official channels, or offered adjusted commission caps. Underperformance may result in coaching, temporary suspension, or termination per the Agreement.
