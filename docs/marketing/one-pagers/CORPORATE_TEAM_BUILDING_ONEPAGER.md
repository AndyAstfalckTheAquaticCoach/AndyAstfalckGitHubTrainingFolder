# The Aquatic Coach Platform  
## Corporate Team Building — Decision Maker One-Pager

**Version 1.0 · For HR, People & Culture, and Marketing leaders**

---

### THE OFFER IN ONE SENTENCE

Your company receives a **free, coach-led team running program** (up to 15 employees) building toward a shared event goal — in return for **weekly social media posts** from your corporate account and **5 selected team members**.

---

### AT A GLANCE

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost to your business** | **€0** platform fee (value-exchange partnership) |
| **Team size** | Up to **15 participants** |
| **Social advocates** | **5 selected employees** (“Platform 5”) + **1 corporate account** |
| **Program length** | Typically **12–16 weeks** to target event |
| **Ideal company size** | Medium enterprise (**50–250 employees**) |
| **Target event** | Corporate 5K / 10K / half marathon / charity run / relay |

---

### WHAT YOUR TEAM GETS (FREE)

- Structured **team running program** aligned to your event date  
- **Evidence-based coaching** via The Aquatic Coach Platform (Intervals.icu integrated)  
- **Readiness-aware training** — go / modify / skip based on load and recovery  
- **Group run programming** — up to 2 organised sessions/month  
- **Race-week guidance** — taper, pacing, pre-event checklist  
- **Onboarding** for up to 15 employees  

---

### WHAT YOUR BUSINESS PROVIDES (VALUE EXCHANGE)

| When | Posts required |
|------|----------------|
| **Every week** | 1 **corporate** post + 1 post each from **5 Platform 5** members = **6 posts/week** |
| **Race day** | 1 pre-race post (corporate) + 5 pre-race posts (Platform 5) = **6 posts** |
| **Within 48h after race** | 1 finish-line post (corporate) + 5 finish-line posts (Platform 5) = **6 posts** |
| **Media assets** | Photos/video from **≥2 group training runs** (min. 10 images or 2 video clips) |

**12-week total:** ~**84 posts** + race-day content

---

### PLATFORM 5 — THE 5 SELECTED ADVOCATES

Each nominee must:

- Participate actively in the running program  
- Post **once per week** on personal social media (LinkedIn, Instagram, Strava, etc.)  
- Publish **pre-race** and **finish-line** content on event day  
- Attend **≥2 group training runs** (for media/content)  
- Include **partnership disclosure** on every post  

*Remaining participants (up to 15 total) train on the platform but are not required to post weekly.*

---

### MANDATORY DISCLOSURE (ALL POSTS)

### COMPLIANCE & GOOD STANDING

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|------------|
| 1 missed post / week | Reminder + catch-up within 7 days |
| 2+ misses in 4 weeks | Written notice + corrective plan |
| Missing race-day / finish-line posts | **Material breach** — may end free access |
| Good standing | Renewal invite, case study feature, extended caps |

Fortnightly post summary sent to your HR/Marketing contact.
